# Website fonts

Self-hosted fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. No Google Fonts requests are made by visitors.

- Source Sans 3 Regular and Semibold: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans/tree/release/WOFF2/TTF
- Source Serif 4 Regular: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif/tree/release/WOFF2/TTF
- Academic Serif SC: renamed character subset of Noto Serif CJK SC Regular, https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Serif/OTF/SimplifiedChinese

Licenses are stored beside the font files. The profile pages currently use Source Sans 3 Regular and Semibold for headings, body text, and navigation, with platform sans-serif fallbacks for Chinese. Source Serif 4 and the Academic Serif SC subset remain available for other typography choices but are not requested by the profile layout.

Typography: body 17px on desktop and 16px on mobile; homepage research titles 20px / 18px; major section headings 25px / 23px. Metadata and secondary actions use 14–15px text.

Spacing: body line-height 1.65 (1.8 for Chinese), publication titles 1.4–1.45, and expanded research introductions 1.7. Biography paragraphs have 12px gaps; homepage research entries are separated by 32px on desktop and 28px on mobile.
